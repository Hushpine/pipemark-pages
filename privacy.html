Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 4, 2026

PipeMark does not collect personal information.

The app calculates conduit bend marks on your phone. Nothing you type is sent to Hushpine.

What stays on your phone

  • The last measurements you entered.
  • How many free calculations you have used today, for each bend.
  • Whether this phone has unlocked the one-time purchase.

That information never leaves the device. Uninstalling PipeMark deletes it.

Purchases

The $4.99 unlock is a one-time purchase handled by Apple or Google. Hushpine does not receive your name, email address, or payment card number. The store only tells the app whether the purchase succeeded.

Buying and restoring a purchase need a network connection. The bend math does not.

What this app does not do

  • No account and no sign-up.
  • No advertising.
  • No analytics and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No sale or sharing of data. There is no data to sell.
  • No access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, or photos.

Children

PipeMark is a job-site tool for electricians. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new date will appear at the top of this page.
