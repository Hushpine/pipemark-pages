Mark the pipe. Then bend.

PipeMark tells you where to put the sharpie on EMT conduit. Inches and fractions, the way a tape reads. For electricians in the United States and Canada.

Three bends

  • Offset. Height of the obstacle, distance from the end of the pipe, and the angle on your bender.
  • Stub 90. How tall the rise should finish, minus the take-up stamped on your bender.
  • 3-point saddle. Obstacle height and the distance to its center.

The big numbers are the marks, measured from the end of the pipe. A second line shows the total inches when a mark is 12 inches or more.

The same numbers as the Klein card

Multipliers and shrink match the Klein Tools conduit bender card. Results round to the nearest 1/16 inch. If your bender's take-up stamp is different, enter that stamp. It wins.

Price

Each bend includes 3 free calculations a day. One purchase of $4.99 unlocks unlimited calculations for all three bends. No subscription. No ads. No account.

No signal required

The math runs on the phone. A dead zone does not change the marks. Buying and restoring the purchase are the only steps that need a connection.
